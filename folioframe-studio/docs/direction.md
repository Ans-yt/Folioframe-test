# Direction contract

## Decision context
The user delegated the end-to-end concept, then confirmed creative clients as the portfolio audience, plain HTML/CSS/JS, and code-first. The implementation is a product decision, not validated user research. No image-generation service is used.

## Grounded directions considered
1. Photograph mounting bench — mount, inspect, and finish one work at a time.
2. Printer's color specification sheet — precise settings next to one decisive proof.
3. Creative agency contact sheet — compare art directions at thumbnail scale.
4. Architectural portfolio flat plan — proportion, margins, and hierarchy lead.
5. Browser inspection panel — familiar precise controls, but risks looking developer-only.
6. Video grading suite — a dominant monitor and instrument-like controls, but dark by category.
7. Contemporary gallery proof room — a luminous working surface, generous framed proof, and a compact preparation rail.

Seed `29292a82` assigned candidate 7. It is the built direction. The product is used in daylight while preparing presentations, so the workspace is light, with dark ink, restrained blue control states, and a lilac proof carrying the expressive color. This is an Operate surface; the image leads, controls stay conventional.

## Challenger judgments
The roll returned six challengers. They were judged on audience identification and product clarity, not novelty alone. None beats a focused proof room on both.
- Cloud quarry: declined. Cutting weather is unrelated to composition; retain disciplined separation of solid working zones.
- Industrial quote grammar: declined. It adds a fashion identity the product does not need; retain literal action labels and unambiguous active states.
- eBoy pixel metropolis: declined. It communicates exploration instead of image preparation; retain precision at every output resolution.
- Boarding pass / gate board: competitive on clarity, weaker on creative identification; retain stable control positions when states change.
- HyperCard shoebox: competitive on direct manipulation, weaker on output fidelity; retain immediate visual feedback and a reversible edit history.
- Rhythm machine: declined. Temporal controls misrepresent a still-image tool; retain tactile preset buttons without importing instrument decoration.

## Committed first viewport
An ink wordmark and sparse utility header sit above a two-column studio. The left has a short oversized headline and a large lilac 4:3 artboard containing an original website example; the right has a white preparation rail. A low sample strip offers three genuine renderable examples. The export action is always identifiable in the header. Mobile stacks the proof above controls and keeps export reachable.

## Signature interaction
Changing an art direction recomposes the same screenshot and lettering on a single canvas. The downloaded PNG is painted by that same renderer at 2× resolution, not a screenshot of the UI. Undo/redo reverses composition edits. All sample artwork is generated locally and marked fictional.

## Honest risk
The restrained workspace deliberately lets the exported compositions carry the visual expression. It will not have the cinematic movement of a marketing microsite. That trade favors a useful tool and a credible creative-client handoff.

## Finish criterion
Working portable output, visible error recovery, desktop/mobile inspection, an independent review, recorded built design system, and no fictitious commercial proof. No raster assets are generated by an image model.
